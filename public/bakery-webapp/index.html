Our Story

A neighbourhood bakery since 2011

Sugar & Crumb began as a two-oven kitchen run by a mother-and-daughter duo. Today we bake over a hundred trays a day, yet every loaf is still shaped by hand and every cake is still frosted to order.

  • 🌾 Fresh ingredients sourced from local farms
  • 👩‍🍳 100% handmade, small-batch production
  • 🏅 Quality promise — baked today or it's on us
  • 🎂 Custom cakes for birthdays and weddings
